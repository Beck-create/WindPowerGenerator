# WindPower Generator

A conversion of energy which mean convert electricity by the motion of the blade but not produce motion of the blade by electricity.



###### Introduce to the demo picture:



Use bearing to connect the upper and lower part, place generator and the three-phase rectifier (to convert the three-phase AC to DC )on upper part. Use M8 screw to fix this thing on a rail across the hole on the lower part.



The sector perpendicular to the upper part is used to correct the direction of the generator by the wind.



##### To DEMO:



I connected a LED with resistor to the rectifier to show the electricity generated out.



##### Noticing:



The voltage generated can be very high some time ,soo be careful when it is working.



Remember to check the parameters like my rectifier get a maximum 50A and 1200V ,my generator will generate only 261V under 9950RPM(by seller's test data)  ,it is already extremely high in natural environment .The largest current is around 200mA which is 250 times lower than 50A ,so it is very safe.

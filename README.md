# WindPowerGenerator

A conversion of energy which mean convert electricity by the motion of the blade but not produce motion of the blade by electricity.





###### Introduce to the demo picture:



Use bearing to connect the upper and lower part, place generator on upper part ,and a platform on the rod to place the three-phase rectifier to convert the three-phase AC to DC. Use M8 screw to fix this thing on a rail across the hole on the lower part.
